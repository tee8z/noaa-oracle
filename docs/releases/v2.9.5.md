# v2.9.5 — Keep a report's other values when its rain amount is implausible

This release changes settlement inputs.
The oracle's rain-amount bound (a negative or non-finite amount, or more than 5 in in one report) used to reject the whole report.
That discarded the report's temperature, dewpoint and wind, and held every event at the station for review, including events that score only temperature and wind.
The bound now rejects the `precipitation` group only, as the daemon's own precipitation flags already do.
The rejected amount is dropped, never clamped or replaced.
The report's temperature, dewpoint and wind count toward settlement and coverage.
Events that score rain or snow, or every metric, are still held for review, as before.
Every other screen (units, physical bounds, temperature jumps, publication conflicts) still affects every group, and eligibility still leaves out any rejected report.
`GET /stations/observation-quality` counts such a report only when the requested metrics include rain or snow, or when no metrics are given.

A coverage block now names every station short of the observation window.
Settlement used to stop at the first short station in id order, so a block could name one station while others were also short.
A single short station gives exactly the block it gave before; several give one `source_unavailable` block with a reason per station.
A station with no reports at all is named beside the others, and on its own is still left to the readings check (`incomplete_readings`).
Which events settle, and when, does not change.
