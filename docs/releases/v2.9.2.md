# v2.9.2 — Reduce query result duplication

Forecast, daily weather, station-list, and eligibility timeline reads decode Arrow batches as they arrive.
Decoded output remains available while completed batches are released.
Eligibility reports accumulate directly into the retained timeline, without an intermediate timestamp-vector copy.
Forecast quality totals and range diagnostics merge across every batch.
Some multi-pass aggregate queries still retain their result batches.

Processing, file preparation, and eligibility warming wait for exclusive heavy-work admission.
The two-minute diagnostic interval no longer permits them to bypass the shared budget.
A delayed pass keeps its FIFO queue position and logs its wait every two minutes.
Shutdown cancels the wait.
Long queries can therefore delay a processing pass; existing stale-value and readiness monitoring remain relevant.

Tests compare multiple batches of forecasts and quality records, verify timeline ordering, and cover exclusive background admission.
The change reduces application-side result duplication.
It does not impose a complete process-memory cap or remove DuckDB’s internal query allocations.
