# v2.9.7 — Settle a pair its reports already decide

This release changes settlement inputs.
v2.9.6 tolerates sensor outages of up to 4 hours of a day. A longer outage still held an event until it expired unsigned, even when the reports there were had already decided the pair's line.

For an event scored against lines, missing reports can only raise a maximum and lower a minimum.
A high temperature or strongest wind whose miss is already above its line's upper cut is therefore Over, and a low temperature whose miss is already below the lower cut is Under, whatever the missing reports read.
Such a pair now settles through any outage, on the extreme of the reports there are.
A miss on a cut is Par and stays undecided, a station's temperatures settle this way only when every scored temperature of it is decided, and wind direction and accumulations are never decided this way.
Events scored by the fixed rules hold as before.

Settlement now reads an event's lines before its observations, to pass each pair's cuts and baseline to the reader.
