# v2.9.6 — Settle through the sensor outages storms bring

This release changes settlement inputs.
Small automated stations lose their wind or temperature for one to four hours in snow or freezing fog.
Settlement held any event whose usable reports, for any of its station and metric pairs, left a gap over 90 minutes; a window of 12 hours or longer tolerated one gap of up to 150 minutes, and none at its end.
Such an outage expired the event unsigned and refunded every entry.

A window of 12 hours or longer now tolerates gaps, per station and per metric and the window end included, of up to a sixth of the window (4 hours of a day, 2 of half a day), while the gaps over 90 minutes cover no more than a quarter of it together (6 hours of a day).
Over the stations competitions used in October 2026, 99.6% of day windows fell within these limits, against 97.5% before; the rest were outages lasting days.
A tolerated gap adds no value for the hours missed: the high, low and strongest wind come from the reports either side, and an accumulation across the gap stays unavailable.
Windows shorter than 12 hours keep the 90-minute rule.

Station selection (`GET /stations/eligible`) keeps the stricter rule, so the stations offered are ones whose recent windows settled without an outage.

Events held by such a gap settle at their next retry.
